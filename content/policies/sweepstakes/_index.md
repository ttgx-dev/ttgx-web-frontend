Owners, employees, and immediate family members of Trove Expo Group, LLC are not eligible to win.

Void in California, Rhode Island, and where prohibited by law.

**3\. How to Enter**

Eligible participants may enter through either of the following methods, subject to the entry limits described below.

**Badgeholder Entries**

Eligible TTGX badgeholders will automatically receive Sweepstakes entries as follows:

| Badge Type | Convention-Wide Entries | Monthly Entries |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Friday | 1 | 1 per eligible month |
| Saturday | 1 | 1 per eligible month |
| Sunday | 1 | 1 per eligible month |
| Weekend | 4 | 1 per eligible month |
| Deluxe | 5 | 1 per eligible month |
| All-In | 6 | 1 per eligible month |

For monthly drawings, an eligible badgeholder will receive one (1) entry for each drawing whose entry period ends on or after the date of their eligible badge purchase.

Badgeholders who purchased an eligible badge before the Sweepstakes began will receive one (1) entry in each monthly drawing for which they remain eligible.

Badge purchases made after the applicable drawing's entry deadline will not generate entries for that drawing.

Badge upgrades, refunds, cancellations, and transfers will be reconciled to ensure that each eligible participant receives the appropriate number of entries without exceeding the applicable entry limits. Entries obtained independently through the free online entry method will not be affected by subsequent badge refunds or cancellations.

**Free Online Entry \- No Purchase Necessary**

Eligible participants may enter the Sweepstakes without purchasing a badge or any other product by completing the free online entry form at:

[https://click.ttgexpo.com/sweep](https://click.ttgexpo.com/sweep)

Each valid form submission provides one (1) entry into the drawing selected by the participant.

Participants may submit the form multiple times, subject to a maximum of six (6) total entries into the convention-wide drawing and one (1) entry into each monthly drawing, regardless of entry method.

For monthly drawings, participants must select "Current Month's Drawing" on the entry form. Entries will be assigned to the monthly drawing corresponding to the calendar month in which the form was submitted, as determined by the submission timestamp recorded by TTGX in Central Time.

Monthly entries must be submitted during the applicable monthly entry period specified in Section 2\. Entries submitted outside the published monthly entry periods will not be eligible for a monthly drawing.

Convention-wide drawing entries must be submitted by 11:59 PM Central Time on April 15, 2027\.

Participants who have already received entries through another method may submit additional free entries only until they reach the applicable maximum entry limit.

No purchase or TTGX badge is required to enter or win.

**4\. Entry Limits and Winner Selection**

Each eligible person may receive a maximum of six (6) entries into the convention-wide drawing and one (1) entry into each monthly drawing, regardless of entry method.

Entries exceeding these limits will be disregarded.

All valid entries have an equal chance of being selected. Odds of winning depend on the total number of eligible entries received for each drawing.

Monthly drawing entries do not carry over to subsequent monthly drawings or the convention-wide drawing.

Winners will be selected randomly from all eligible entries received by the applicable deadline.

A participant may win a maximum of one (1) prize in the convention-wide drawing. Once selected as a winner, that participant's remaining entries will be excluded from subsequent prize selections in the convention-wide drawing.

Winning a monthly prize does not affect eligibility for subsequent monthly drawings or the convention-wide drawing.

**5\. Winner Notification and Prize Claims**

**Winner Notification and Confirmation**

Winners will be notified using the email address associated with their Sweepstakes entry. Winners are responsible for monitoring their email, including spam and junk folders.

Monthly drawing winners must confirm their acceptance of their prize and intention to collect it no later than 11:59 PM Central Time on the seventh calendar day following the date their winning notification email is sent.

Convention-wide drawing winners must confirm their acceptance of their prize no later than 11:59 PM Central Time on Friday, April 16, 2027\.

To confirm acceptance, winners must reply to their winning notification email and indicate their intention to collect their prize.

**Alternate Winner Selection**

If a potential winner is ineligible, declines a prize, or fails to confirm their acceptance by the applicable deadline, TTGX will randomly select an alternate winner from the remaining eligible entries for the applicable drawing.

Alternate winners will be notified by email and must confirm their acceptance within seven (7) calendar days of notification, unless an earlier confirmation deadline is specified in their notification and is reasonably necessary to facilitate prize collection during the convention.

If an alternate winner is ineligible, declines the prize, or fails to confirm acceptance by the applicable deadline, TTGX will continue selecting alternate winners from the remaining eligible entries until the prize has been awarded or no eligible entrants remain available and willing to accept it, subject to applicable law.

The limit of one (1) convention-wide prize per participant applies to alternate winners as well as originally selected winners.

**Prize Collection**

All prizes will be available for pickup at TTGX between April 16 and April 18, 2027\.

Prizes must be collected no later than 3:00 PM Central Time on Sunday, April 18, 2027, at:

Heritage Center of Brooklyn Center  
6155 Earle Brown Drive  
Brooklyn Center, MN 55430

Winners do not need to purchase a TTGX badge to claim their prizes. Complimentary access will be provided for prize pickup.

Winners must present their winning notification email and may be required to provide reasonable proof of identity and eligibility when claiming their prize.

Confirming acceptance does not extend the prize collection deadline unless alternative arrangements are expressly approved by TTGX.

**Unclaimed Prizes and Post-Convention Arrangements**

If a confirmed winner fails to collect their prize by 3:00 PM Central Time on April 18, 2027, and has not made alternative arrangements with TTGX in advance, the prize will be forfeited, subject to applicable law.

TTGX will continue selecting and contacting alternate winners from the remaining eligible entries for the applicable drawing after the convention, as necessary, to facilitate the awarding of any unclaimed prizes.

Alternate winners selected after the convention will be notified by email and must confirm their acceptance within seven (7) calendar days of notification.

For prizes awarded after the convention, TTGX will coordinate alternative pickup arrangements with the alternate winner.

TTGX will continue making reasonable efforts to award all advertised prizes, including contacting additional alternate winners when necessary, until all prizes have been awarded or no eligible entrants remain available and willing to accept them, subject to applicable law.
