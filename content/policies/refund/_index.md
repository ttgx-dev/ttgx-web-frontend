Badge refunds are available up to one (1) day prior to the first day of TTGX, subject to a six percent (6%) processing fee. Refunds are issued to the original payment method.

For TTGX 2027 (April 16-18, 2027), the refund deadline is April 15, 2027.

## Illness exception

To encourage attendees to stay home when sick, TTGX may, at its discretion, provide either (a) a refund to the original payment method subject to a six percent (6%) processing fee, or (b) an event credit for a future TTGX. Requests must be submitted prior to badge pickup and prior to attending the event, and may require a brief self-attestation. TTGX may deny requests where there is evidence of badge use, attendance, or policy abuse.

## No-shows

No-shows are not eligible for refunds.

## Event cancellation and force majeure

If TTGX cancels the event, TTGX will issue full refunds for attendee badges. If TTGX is unable to proceed due to events beyond TTGX's reasonable control (force majeure), TTGX will provide attendees with credit equal to the amount paid (including any processing fees) toward a future TTGX event.
