## Play Together

Alongside the open game library and exhibit hall, TTGX will host scheduled events throughout the convention, April 16-18, 2027. Expect scheduled board game sessions, tournaments, card games, big-game events, giveaways, and creative community-led activities. Events are hosted by attendees, exhibitors, and other members of the tabletop community.

Events are scheduled time slots for a specific activity. Most are included with convention admission; an event may charge an additional fee when it includes materials or take-home items.

## Join an Event

Event registration opens January 15, 2027, through [Tabletop.Events](https://tabletop.events/conventions/twin-cities-tabletop-games-expo). Browse the schedule and register for events that interest you. Some events have limited seats, and some may have an additional fee. Attendees with a 3 Day All-In badge receive early event signup.

## Host an Event

Have a game or activity you would like to share? Anyone can propose an event for TTGX, from a scheduled board game session to another tabletop-friendly activity. Events require TTGX approval before they can be added to the schedule.

[Submit an event on Tabletop.Events](https://tabletop.events/conventions/twin-cities-tabletop-games-expo/submit-events).

- Submit by **November 13, 2026** for priority schedule placement and maximum visibility to attendees.
- Submissions are accepted through **February 19, 2027**, but late submissions are not guaranteed placement or promotion.
- TTGX will contact hosts with finalized event placement by **January 8, 2027**.
- Event registration opens to attendees on **January 15, 2027**.

To host, create a Tabletop.Events account and purchase a badge for the day or days you will be running events. Hosts who run at least **32 player-hours** of events are eligible for a badge refund. Player-hours are calculated as the event's maximum player seats multiplied by its scheduled duration in hours; the host does not count as a player. For example, eight hours of four-player games totals 32 player-hours. After hosting, submit the event host refund form to receive the badge cost back.

Review the [Event Host Policy](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1-2dykXWk1f_9MBjL9WNC7ZEKNp6N-mX_/view?usp=sharing) before submitting. Hosts may participate in their own event, but if it sells out and they have not reserved a seat, they are expected to run it as the game master only. Arrive at your assigned table 10 minutes before your event, tidy the space afterward, and leave it ready for the next event.
