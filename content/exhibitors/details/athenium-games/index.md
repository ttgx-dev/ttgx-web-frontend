Athenium Games creates tabletop games that explore topics well suited to both the classroom and family game night. Designed to entertain and enlighten curious minds from ages 10 to 102, its games aim to spark new connections, passions, and ideas.

Founded in 2019 by Dan Besemann, Athenium Games grew from his more than 20 years of teaching and developing integrated curricula at Hamline University. With a Ph.D. in chemistry from the University of Wisconsin-Madison, Dan saw a natural link between curriculum and game design and set out to bring the benefits of game-based learning to a wider audience.
