As an enby artist with late-diagnosed AuDHD, I have spent my entire life creating imagery to connect with other humans. After finding my authentic self, I dedicated my life to empowering others to be themselves through my work. I am weird and you are welcome!

I have two businesses in Minnesota: I am a freelance illustrator for fantasy and tabletop RPGs, and I own and run The BirdHive, a sensory-friendly graphic T-shirt company with big goals for changing how society treats disability and neurodiversity in the workforce.
