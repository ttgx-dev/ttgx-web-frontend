Speedrobo Games is a long-running indie tabletop games publisher, developer, distributor, and manufacturer based in Mattoon, Illinois. Speedrobo Games is founded on the principle that if it is not fun, then what is the point. The company prides itself on never leaving a game behind and keeping its games accessible and affordable for players and collectors.
