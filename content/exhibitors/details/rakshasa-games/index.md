### Witches' Quarrel

A fast two-player legacy game about best friends learning magic by hurling spells at each other. Witches' Quarrel is a quick legacy game for two players, where Yi-Feng and Nitya learn magic in 20-minute deck-building duels.

Witches' Quarrel comes in 36 sealed envelopes. The first game starts with just three envelopes and enough spells and instructions to begin playing. As players complete rituals, they open more envelopes, gently adding spells, complexity, and more of Yi-Feng and Nitya's tender story.
