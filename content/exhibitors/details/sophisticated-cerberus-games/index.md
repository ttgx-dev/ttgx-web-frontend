Sophisticated Cerberus Games is a Minnesota-based publisher founded in 2020 by three longtime friends. Their focus is functional spectacle: components that look distinctive because they do distinctive jobs and create table presence players actually use.

In The Stifling Dark, players use acrylic flashlights to track line of sight in a tense one-versus-many hidden-movement horror showdown. In Sprocketforge, players run a magical factory by rotating a network of interlocking gears to time production, storage, and upgrades. Their next release, Lemonade Wars, shifts to a lighter game while keeping the same commitment to theme-forward design.
