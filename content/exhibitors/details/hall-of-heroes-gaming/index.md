Hall of Heroes Gaming, LLC is a board game design studio based in Minneapolis, Minnesota. Founded by a quartet of passionate gamers and creators, we've combined our love of tabletop games with a relentless drive to bring fresh ideas to the world!

Our mission is to create the games we’ve always wanted to play. We strive to create memorable experiences that are imaginative, engaging, and (most importantly) fun to play.
